FAQ

Questions about Kontraq

What is Kontraq and who is it for? +
Kontraq is Nigeria's Business Opportunity Network — a platform where entrepreneurs, SMEs, contractors, and service providers discover real contracts and tenders, while organizations find verified businesses to work with. It covers engineering, ICT, construction, security, education technology, and more across Nigeria.
How is Kontraq different from a freelance marketplace? +
Kontraq is built for serious business contracts — not task-based freelancing. It supports structured proposals, digital contracts, milestone-based payments, deal rooms, business verification, and Trust Scores. The platform is designed for contracts worth hundreds of thousands to hundreds of millions of naira.
What is the Trust Score? +
The Trust Score is a dynamic score (0–100) calculated from your business verification status, completed contract history, client ratings, response rate, and dispute record. It is not a government certification — it is a platform-generated reputation metric based on verified platform activity. It helps clients assess contractors with confidence.
How does the Deal Room work? +
When a client accepts your proposal, a Deal Room is automatically created. Inside, both parties manage the digital contract, milestone progress, secure messaging, documents, and milestone-based payments — all in one protected, auditable space.
How do payments work? +
Payments on Kontraq are processed via Flutterwave in naira — supporting card, bank transfer, USSD and mobile money. Payments are released milestone by milestone as work is confirmed, protecting both the client and the contractor. Full payment history builds your Trust Score automatically.
Is Kontraq only for Nigerian businesses? +
Kontraq starts in Nigeria and is being built to expand across West Africa and the broader African continent. Opportunities are available across Nigeria — Ondo, Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, and beyond. Remote and nationwide opportunities are clearly marked.
